Welcome to Casa Feliz

01

A home in the green heart of La Palma.

Casa Feliz is a spacious, single-level villa in the quiet hills of Breña Alta. Designed as a true home rather than a standard holiday let, it is filled with thoughtful details, original ceramics and places to settle in with a book, a long lunch or a view.

Outside, mature subtropical gardens create complete privacy around a large heated pool, hot tub and a choice of sunny or shaded terraces. From here, look out to the sea on one side and the mountains on the other.
